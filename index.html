About Us

Painting, renovation and property refresh in Saskatoon.

W28 Construction is a Saskatoon-based painting, renovation and property-refresh company focused on quality workmanship, clear communication and practical solutions.

We bring hands-on experience in commercial painting, residential improvements and property renovations. We started W28 to provide homeowners, landlords, property managers, realtors and local businesses with a dependable team for the work that often falls between a simple paint job and a full renovation.

We believe smaller projects deserve the same attention to detail as larger ones. Whether you're preparing a rental for a new tenant, refreshing a home before selling, updating a business space or simply giving your property a new look, we're here to help.
